Warranty Maintenance Obligation Endurance § Your Responsibilities
✅ Key Requirement — Keep All Receipts You must maintain your vehicle per the GM OEM recommended maintenance schedule as outlined in the Owner's Manual. You are required to retain proof of maintenance (repair orders with date, mileage, services performed, shop name & address, VIN, totals, and payment method). These records may be requested in the event of a claim.
⚠ Exclusion — Lack of Maintenance Repairs caused by lack of maintenance, or failure to use manufacturer-specified lubricants, fluids, and coolants are NOT covered. This makes maintaining the documented service record critical for any future claim.
